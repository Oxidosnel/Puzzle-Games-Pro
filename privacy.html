Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Information We Collect

Puzzle Games Pro is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require user registration and collect minimal data:

  • Usage Data — Anonymous page views and game play statistics to improve our service
  • Device Information — Browser type, screen size, and operating system for compatibility optimization
  • Cookies — Essential cookies for site functionality and third-party cookies from advertising partners

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense — For displaying relevant advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out at Google Ad Settings
  • Google Analytics — For understanding site usage patterns. Data is anonymized and aggregated

Data Storage

Game progress may be stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared through your browser settings at any time.

Children's Privacy

Our games are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our site does not require any personal information to use.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Clear all locally stored game data via browser settings

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at privacy@awesomeskills.online
